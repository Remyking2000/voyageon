Amboseli

Elephants and wetlands beneath Kilimanjaro.

Amboseli’s open plains make the scale of the landscape easy to take in. Between the dry ground and the swamps, elephant herds and waterbirds give a game drive two very different settings.

What to experience

Elephant country

Spend time around the swamps, where elephants often gather and feed. A slower drive leaves time to watch how the herd moves between the water and open ground.

Mountain views

Kilimanjaro sits across the border in Tanzania and forms the park’s backdrop when the sky clears. Try an early drive if you want to photograph the mountain in softer light.

Wetlands and birdlife

Pause at wetland viewpoints to scan for birds and other wildlife. The contrast between green marsh and dusty tracks is part of what makes Amboseli worth a longer visit.
