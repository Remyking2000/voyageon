Nairobi National Park

Open grassland on the edge of the capital.

In Nairobi National Park, you can see wildlife against the city skyline. A drive moves between open plains, watercourses and wooded areas, all close enough to fit into a stay in Nairobi.

What to experience

Wildlife and skyline

Look across the open ground for rhinos, giraffes and other grazers. Where the view opens up toward the city, the skyline offers a striking backdrop for wildlife photography.

Where to pause

Take time near the park’s water and riverine areas. They give you a change of scenery from the plains and a chance to look for birds.

Fitting it into Nairobi

Set aside a morning for a drive and leave enough time for travel across the city. Check the park’s official entry information as you plan your visit.
