Our approach

Start with the part of Kenya you most want to experience. Give it enough time, then decide which other stops fit the pace you want.

How to shape your trip

Start with a place

Choose the experience you’d travel for, whether that’s wild mornings in the Mara, wildlife against Nairobi’s skyline, or time beside Kilifi Creek.

Set a comfortable pace

Think about when you like to start your day and how much time you want between outings. Allow space in your plans for travel, meals, and rest.

Share what matters

When you get in touch, tell us your travel dates, group size, places you’re interested in, and any practical needs so we know where to begin.

Tell us what you have in mind

Send us a message on Instagram with a few details about the trip you want to take.
