Kilifi

A tidal creek, quiet beaches and Swahili history.

Kilifi sits where a tidal creek meets the Indian Ocean. The creek, the beach and the ruins above the water are close enough to explore at an easy pace over a couple of days.

What to experience

On the creek

Take in the shoreline from a paddle or a sail. The creek looks different as the tide turns, so ask locally about suitable conditions before settling on a time to go out.

Mnarani ruins

Above the water, the remains of mosques and tombs mark a former Swahili settlement. Walk the site slowly and look for the stonework beneath the baobabs.

Bofa Beach

Spend some unhurried time on Kilifi’s ocean side. A beach afternoon pairs well with a morning at Mnarani or a late sail on the creek.
