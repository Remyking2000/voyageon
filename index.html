Destinations in Kenya

Find your kind of out there.

Follow wildlife across the savanna, spend a morning on safari just outside Nairobi, or make your way to the Indian Ocean.

Southwestern Kenya · Safari

Maasai Mara

Game drives take you across open grassland and down toward the Mara River. Early mornings and late afternoons give you two different views of the same landscape, with time at camp in between.

For a first safari, spend a few nights here so you can explore different parts of the reserve at an easy pace.

  • Game drives
  • Mara River
  • Savanna camps
Explore Maasai Mara ↗

Southern Kenya · Safari

Amboseli

Elephant herds move between dusty plains and wetlands fed by water from Kilimanjaro. On clear mornings, the mountain rises behind the park and gives the landscape its familiar silhouette.

Leave time around the swamps to watch the herds and look for waterbirds along the edges.

  • Elephant herds
  • Kilimanjaro views
  • Wetlands
Explore Amboseli ↗

Nairobi · Safari

Nairobi National Park

On Nairobi’s doorstep, open grassland gives way to wooded river valleys. On parts of a game drive, you can see the city skyline beyond the plains.

A morning here fits naturally into a stay in the capital. Look for rhinos on the plains and birds near the water.

  • Morning game drives
  • Rhino habitat
  • City skyline
Explore Nairobi National Park ↗

Kenyan coast · Heritage

Mombasa

Walk through the narrow streets of Old Town, then follow the harbour to Fort Jesus, the UNESCO-listed fortress overlooking the Indian Ocean.

Give yourself a morning for the fort and the neighbourhood, then spend the afternoon on the coast or stop for a Swahili meal.

  • Old Town
  • Fort Jesus
  • Swahili food
Explore Mombasa ↗

Kenyan coast · Creek and beach

Kilifi

Kilifi Creek opens into the Indian Ocean beside a quieter stretch of coast. See the creek from the water on a paddle or a sunset sail, or spend the afternoon on Bofa Beach.

Above the creek, the Mnarani ruins offer a look at the area’s Swahili history and a view across the water.

  • Kilifi Creek
  • Bofa Beach
  • Mnarani ruins
