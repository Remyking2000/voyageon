Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Voyageon Tours handles information when you visit this website or contact us through the Instagram account linked here.

Using the website

You can browse this site without creating an account. The site has no booking, payment or enquiry forms and does not use first-party cookies or analytics scripts. A hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, requested page and browser details to deliver the site, diagnose problems and protect the service.

Your browser requests typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives technical details such as your IP address and browser information to serve those files. See Google Fonts’ privacy information.

Messages and external links

If you message Voyageon Tours on Instagram, we can see your profile name and the information you share with us. We use it to respond to your message and follow up on your enquiry. Please leave out payment card details and identity documents when messaging us.

Instagram and the other external sites linked here handle information under their own privacy policies.

Your rights and how to contact us

Under Kenyan data protection law, you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of personal information about you, or to object to its processing. To ask a privacy question or make a request, send an initial message to @voyageonstours so we can arrange a suitable way to respond.

You can also file a complaint with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
